Legal & privacy

About this project & contact

Flight Radio Quiz is an independent study app. It is not operated, approved, or endorsed by the Bundesnetzagentur. Project information and contact: FlightRadioQuiz on GitHub. Use the repository’s Issues section to report errors or raise questions about the content.

Question sources & attribution

Question catalogs and reproduced question illustrations: © Bundesnetzagentur für Elektrizität, Gas, Telekommunikation, Post und Eisenbahnen. This app uses these catalogs, valid from 1 May 2024:

  • Prüfungsfragen BZF II und BZF I — 261 questions.
  • Prüfungsfragen AZF und AZF E — 289 questions.

For current catalogs and official examination information, visit the Bundesnetzagentur’s Flugfunk page.

Copyright & license information

The Bundesnetzagentur’s copyright notice states that its website content is generally available under Creative Commons Attribution–NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-ND 4.0), unless otherwise marked. Applicable source-specific notices and third-party rights remain in effect.

CC BY-ND allows redistribution with attribution and a license link, but does not authorize distribution of adapted material. A change of format alone is not an adaptation. This notice does not establish that every part of this app’s use has been separately authorized.

Presentation changes in this app: PDF text is extracted and reflowed, recurring page headers and footers are omitted, question illustrations are extracted, and questions and answer choices are shuffled. The source catalogs identify answer A as correct; the app tracks that answer when shuffling. Extraction errors are possible. No translation is applied. Rights in source material remain separate from rights in the app’s own code.

Study use & accuracy

This app supports exam preparation. It does not replace official training, current regulations, or the official examination. Only the Bundesnetzagentur’s current catalogs and examination requirements are authoritative. The displayed catalog edition and simulated exam settings may differ from current official requirements. Check the official sources before your exam. Do not use this app for operational flight decisions.

Privacy & browser storage

No account is required. The app stores practice statistics, active exams, completed exam reviews, and your theme preference in this browser’s local storage. The app does not upload your answers or results to a server and does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Use “Reset pool statistics” in Statistics to delete the selected pool’s practice data and completed exams; a running exam remains saved. To remove all app data, including active exams and theme preferences, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Browser data does not sync between devices and may be removed by private browsing or browser cleanup.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives connection information and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This app loads the DM Sans and Space Grotesk typefaces through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to request their stylesheets and font files, sending connection information such as your IP address. See Google Fonts privacy information. Opening an external source link also connects you to that website, whose privacy terms apply.
